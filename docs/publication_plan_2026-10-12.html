1Bottom line

The pre-registered analysis is finished, and its biological answer is no. Senescence-gene classifiers for type 2 diabetes did not hold up in independent cohorts in either tissue, and the two tissues share no hub gene. The study is still publishable. Its value lies in how the analysis was done and in what it revealed about the public data.

  • Islet: honest AUC 0.66 (95% CI 0.53–0.79), then 0.59 (0.43–0.74) in an independent cohort. Adipose: 0.53 (0.38–0.68), then 0.44 (0.14–0.74). Every external interval includes 0.5.
  • Data finding: GSE38642 is 63 of the same arrays as GSE41762, byte for byte, and 11 of those donors carry a different diabetes label in each deposit. Published papers still treat the two as independent cohorts.
  • Novelty is real but narrower than first claimed. A 2018 paper already noted the overlap, and the authors of GSE41762 reported the IL-1 signal behind IL1R1 in 2012. The paper must cite both and claim only what is new.
  • To reach a respected journal, add three pre-registered pieces of work: a map of shared donors across public islet datasets, an audit of how published papers use them, and a direct demonstration of how the common pipeline inflates AUC. Realistic submission date: mid-January 2027.
  • Leave docking out of this paper. No target passed the pre-set rule, and docking onto an unvalidated gene would undercut the paper's main message.

3Results at a glance

Each row is an AUC with its 95% confidence interval. The apparent AUC scores a model on the data that built it, so it is always optimistic. The nested cross-validation AUC is the honest estimate from the discovery cohort. The external rows are cohorts the model never saw.

Planted signalAUC 0.984 · 9 of 10 genesThe pipeline finds real signal when it exists.
  • Both final panels are single genes: IL1R1 in islets (chosen in 42% of folds) and SNAI1 in adipose (46%). Only SERPINE2 (70%) and NLK (52%) cross 50%, and neither is in the final panel.
  • SNAI1 points the opposite way to discovery in both adipose validation sets.
  • Removing one adipose PCA outlier drops the adipose AUC to 0.42 (0.27–0.56). Adding BMI changes neither arm.
  • Islet WGCNA found one T2D-linked module (hubs GPC3 and FGF2, flagged as possibly BMI-confounded). IL1R1 sits in no module.
  • In the single-cell data (GSE81608), IL1R1 is lowly expressed and highest in alpha cells. No gene shows a beta-cell-restricted change, with only 5 to 6 T2D donors per tested cell type.

Verdict

Novelty is moderate and mainly methodological. As the work stands, it suits a journal that judges soundness rather than novelty. Rows 3 and 4 are the strongest lever. If a systematic audit confirms them, the paper becomes evidence about how a whole literature reuses public islet data, which is the kind of finding respected methods and diabetes journals publish.

5Scope of the paper

Duplicated cohorts, conflicting labels and selection bias: a pre-registered test of senescence-gene classifiers for type 2 diabetes in public islet and adipose transcriptomes

Core message: analysed under a locked protocol with nested cross-validation and truly independent validation, senescence-gene classifiers for type 2 diabetes lose most of their apparent accuracy, and some cohorts used as independent validation in the literature are the same arrays.

In the paper

  • Data provenance, overlap checks and the label-conflict analysis
  • The pre-registered nested pipeline, both tissues
  • Locked external validation and the pre-specified sensitivity runs
  • WGCNA and single-cell results as descriptive context
  • The cross-tissue test
  • The three extensions in section 6

Left out

  • Docking and the phytocompound panel. The ligand files stay ready for a later study.
  • The Drosophila extension
  • Any new biomarker claim
  • Any re-tuning of the locked models
For the lab

Docking motivated the original proposal. Reviewers of this paper would ask why a compound was docked onto a gene that failed validation, and leaving docking out protects the paper. If a later study finds a validated target, the naringenin, embelin and rutin structures are already in ligands/, and the LSC structure is still needed from the lab.

9Risks and how to handle them

RiskResponse

Small cohorts and wide intervals.Write "no evidence of a generalisable classifier at these sample sizes". Never write "no effect".

10Your part in this work

As first author you will be asked to explain and defend every choice in the paper, by your supervisor and by reviewers. Before writing, do three things.

  1. Re-run the pipeline from a clean copy, script by script, reading each script's header and log as you go.
  2. Read the protocol and logs/deviations.md until you can explain each deviation in one sentence.
  3. Practise explaining three ideas in your own words: why gene selection must sit inside cross-validation, why the apparent AUC is optimistic, and why GSE38642 cannot validate GSE41762.

Your contributions to claim under CRediT are conceptualisation, methodology (the study design and its decisions), interpretation and writing. The formal analysis and software were run with AI assistance under your direction. The AI-use statement must say so, and you remain responsible for both.
